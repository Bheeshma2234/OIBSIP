# Task 5 – Research Report: Social Engineering Attacks

## Objective

The objective of this task is to study various social engineering attacks, understand how attackers manipulate human psychology to gain unauthorized access to systems or confidential information, and learn effective prevention techniques.

---

## About the Task

Social engineering attacks exploit human behavior rather than technical vulnerabilities. Attackers use deception, trust, fear, or curiosity to trick victims into revealing sensitive information or performing actions that compromise security.

This report explains the most common social engineering attacks, their working principles, real-world case studies, and recommended security practices for individuals and organizations.

---

The report includes:
